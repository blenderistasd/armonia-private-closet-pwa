# 100 photographic demo garments

Source: https://github.com/alexeygrigorev/clothing-dataset
License: CC0-1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Original dataset metadata includes image ID, sender ID, class label and kids flag. Photos are NOT current retail catalog SKUs, nor do they imply exact brands, materials or commercial availability.
The complete source of every photo is recorded in photodemo_meta.json. Occasion and style tags are demonstration metadata, not certified product properties. Photos are local WebP thumbnails imported into encrypted IndexedDB by explicit user action only.
